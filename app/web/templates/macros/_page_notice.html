{# Reusable product-facing notice — the quiet "this part is still being finished" caveat that sits under a page's header, below the description. It is deliberately NOT an alert. The copy is advisory (nothing to act on, nothing broken), so the component carries exactly one amber signal: a half-strength wash of --ds-warn-bg over the page surface, a soft hairline, and a single small glyph. The heading stays --ds-text-primary — a warn-coloured heading is what makes a note read as a developer error — and the body sits at --ds-text-secondary, so the eye still lands on the page content below rather than on the caveat. page_notice('Library content is still being prepared', 'Some items and information may be incomplete …') `badge='WIP'` adds a small uppercase marker after the title, for a notice that stands in for a named thing which does not exist yet (e.g. "Data apps"). It is a flat label, not a filled chip: inside a panel this quiet, a bordered pill would outshout the sentence it qualifies. Consecutive notices stack with their own 10px rhythm; the last one in a page head is flushed by the shell's `.idx-head > :last-child` rule. Styling lives in style-custom.css under the `.pnote-*` prefix — shared, so no page carries per-page notice CSS. #} {% import "macros/_icon.html" as _pn_ico %} {% macro page_notice(title, copy, badge=None, label=None) %}
